Second alpha of GTNH Planner: plan GT New Horizons factories in game, from NEI.

**This is an alpha.** Expect rough edges, and keep a backup of your world's planner saves (`gtnhplanner/plans.dat`, backed up automatically to `backups/` beside it). Bugs and ideas: GTNH Planner's thread on the GT New Horizons Discord (the Discord key on the planner's top bar).

**Made for GTNH 2.9.** This build is made against GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2's mods (NEI 2.8.155, ModularUI2 2.3.92, GTNHLib 0.11.52, GT5-Unofficial 5.09.54). NEI changed in 2.9, so it is not for GTNH 2.8; stay on 0.2.0-alpha.1 there.

## New

- **Share key** on the planner's top bar: share the open plan to the library, copy its plan code, or take a screenshot of it.
- **Screenshots of a plan**: a picture of the whole plan with Detailed or Simple cards, and if you like its name and what goes in and out underneath. Save it to your screenshots folder, or copy it and paste it anywhere.
- **Your library posts**: give a post an icon and a description. Sharing a plan again updates its post (votes and downloads stay), and you can edit or delete your own posts from the Library.
- **Sound**: the planner has its own sounds, with a Sounds setting (Off, Quiet, Normal, Loud).
- **Custom rate cards**: a source or a drain you dial by hand, under Non-recipe.
- **Settings** in a box of their own, with a See-through slider for the board.
- **The tour** is offered the first time the planner opens, and now shows byproducts and trash.

## Changed

- Press anything on the board and drag to move it, or let go to click it. What you move never lands on another card or drawer, and glides into place.
- A wire in hand outlines what would take it, snaps onto the port it would go to, and turns red where letting go would do nothing.
- Hovering a port, drawer or wire lights only what it is wired to.
- Drawers are slimmer, in the card's look, with a shape for each kind.
- The overview's machine counts can be set from the overview; trash no longer counts as an output.
- Tooltips wait a moment before they show; tier chips are flat and crisp.
- Placing a card in the world turns the plan over the world on.

## Fixed

- GTNH 2.9: opening a recipe page, or drawing the first card, crashed the game.
- Far zoomed out, a click on a card could start a wire from a hidden port, or open NEI.
- Sticky notes: their keys show at any zoom, there is a delete key, and resizing and text size behave.

## Install

1. Drop `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-alpha.2.jar` into your GTNH 2.9 instance's `mods` folder, replacing any older `gtnhplanner` jar (client only; servers don't need it).
2. Open your inventory: the GTNH Planner button is at the bottom left.
