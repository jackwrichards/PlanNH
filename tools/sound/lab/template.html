GTNH Planner Sound Lab

Every sound is made live in your browser from the same recipes the game's files are rendered from. Use the board to hear them in place, switch the variation techniques on and off on the right, and pick between versions further down. When you like a set, copy your picks at the bottom and paste them to me.

Drag from a port to a drawer of the same resource on the matching side (inputs left, outputs right), or onto empty board for a new drawer. Click a wire to cut it. Drag the card by its title. Scroll or click the tier and amps chips (right-click steps down). Pin the machine count, then wire all three inputs to hear the plan start running.
